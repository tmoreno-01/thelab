moving image / computational painting

field studies.

Earlier iterations of the pigment renderer. All versions live on this page. Videos stay unloaded until you choose one, and only one study plays at a time.

Dense optical flow, temporal change and image structure are extracted from source footage, then translated into a separate bristle and pigment simulation. Each version explores a different approach to pigment emission, transport, detail and motion.
