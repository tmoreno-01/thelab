SOURCE ATLAS / ANALYSING IMAGE MATERIAL

006 — the twenty-image source atlas and image-derived contour system, expanded into forty source plates. It builds the drawing through multi-stage fusions between unrelated source contours, fine clipped engraving inside selected forms and nested contour echoes. Placement responds to ink density, existing structures and compositional balance. All movement comes from marks being progressively drawn and transformed; there are no free-floating animated lines or distortion effects. Saved source directions influence future sessions locally. Source images stay on this website and are never uploaded. Engine source.
