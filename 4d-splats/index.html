4D Splat Studio

Load multiple original full_vda ZIPs together. Arrange animated reconstructions as rings, helices, arcs, spheres, grids or loose spatial studies. Adjust each scene and its individual point size, then render a 360° MP4 loop locally.

Each scene has its own overall scale and dot-size control. Click a name to isolate it; tick the box to include or exclude it from the arrangement and MP4.

Render a 360° loop

The camera completes one full revolution while every enabled 4D sequence plays at its source frame rate. Source motion wraps at the end of each clip, so a perfectly seamless motion loop depends on the clip's first and last frames matching. MP4 encoding happens locally; your ZIPs are never uploaded.
