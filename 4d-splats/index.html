4D Splat Studio

Load multiple original full_vda ZIPs together. Arrange animated reconstructions as rings, helices, arcs, spheres, grids or loose spatial studies. Adjust each scene and its individual point size, then render a 360° MP4 loop locally.

Depth reveal / rotation

Only the frontmost splat receives extra depth, with a brief crossfade to its incoming neighbour. Other splats keep their own baseline depth. Handoff controls the transition width; vertical wheels use the bottom of the screen.

Scale, dots, depth and rotation are independent for each scene. Adjust a scene's Depth to override the global cutoff; Custom · reset restores the global setting. Use Move to select a splat, then drag in the viewport. Shift-drag or scroll in Grab mode changes depth. Click a scene name to isolate it.

Render a 360° loop

Your current composition completes one full revolution on a pure black background, without UI. Individual positions, rotations, dot sizes and depth reveal are preserved. Every enabled 4D sequence plays at its source frame rate. Source motion wraps at the end of each clip, so a perfectly seamless motion loop depends on the clip's first and last frames matching. MP4 encoding happens locally; your ZIPs are never uploaded.
